Parallel Lattice Boltzmann Solver (MPI & CUDA)

Parallelized a 2D D2Q9 lattice Boltzmann solver for flow past a cylinder in two ways: MPI slab decomposition with halo exchange (57× on 64 Xeon cores) and a single-GPU CUDA version on a V100. A fused collide–stream kernel reaches ~3,950 MLUPS. Includes strong/weak scaling and roofline analysis.

  • C++
  • MPI
  • CUDA
  • HPC
  • SLURM
